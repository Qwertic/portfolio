# Design System: Qwertic

The full system, the validated theses and the component states live in `MASTER.md` (genjutsu paint, 2026-10-13). This file mirrors its tokens in the front matter so design tooling can read them. When the two disagree, `MASTER.md` wins.

## In one paragraph

A calm, airy single column whose colors follow the real season, in light and dark. Front matter shows autumn light; all eight palettes are in MASTER.md and src/styles/seasons.css. Alegreya Sans at modest sizes, true small caps for metadata, hierarchy by space. Flat components, 1px hairlines, 2 and 4px radii, no shadows, no textures. The strata device (model on top, database as bedrock) carries the layer map, the work cores and the explorer outcomes. One larch-gold fill, reserved for "Book a call". Motion is slow and soft on one curve, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`: 280ms hover, 400ms step, 240ms exit, 480ms layer.
