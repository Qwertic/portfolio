Single source of truth for the redesign. Every color, size, space, radius and duration in code comes from here.

## Theses (validated 2026-10-13)

**Visual.** A calm, airy page in a single readable column whose colors follow the real season (autumn now: fog ground, wet-bark ink, lichen strata fills, one larch-gold accent kept for the call to action), set in Alegreya Sans at modest sizes and regular weights, a humanist face with a handwritten stroke logic that reads like a person rather than a product, with true small caps for metadata; hierarchy by space not scale on an 8px base; flat components with 1px hairlines, 4px radius, no shadows; the strata device (layers ordered like ground, model on top, database as bedrock) is the visual system for the layer map and the decision explorer. Full redesign: nothing of the risograph world survives.

**Interaction.** Slow and soft: hover 280ms color or underline only; layer and state changes 480ms on `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`; in the decision explorer each answered step settles in (opacity plus 8px rise, 400ms) and stays as a visible trail, changing an earlier answer removes later steps with a 240ms fade; the Cairn explainer advances only on click. Scroll draws the structure, never the content (amended and validated 2026-10-13, variant B + C): section rules draw in from the left and strata bands grow top band first, tied to scroll position with native CSS scroll timelines; on wide screens a four-band depth gauge fills as the page is read; text is never hidden or revealed by scroll; unsupported browsers and reduced motion keep the static page. No bounce, parallax, infinite loops, scale on hover or cursor effects. (A flowing generative ground was tried and removed on 2026-10-13: the layer highlight in it was not visible while scrolling.) Everything keyboard reachable; reduced motion makes every change instant.

**Allowed patterns:** scroll-linked drawing of rules and strata bands, and the depth gauge (text is never scroll-revealed); hairline rules; the strata / layer map; the visible decision trail; small-caps metadata labels; source labels ("client work", "own project"); one season line in the footer; the explorer's "uses no model" statement.

## Season rule

Palettes are keyed by `data-season` on `<html>`, set from the visitor's date (northern hemisphere: Dec-Feb winter, Mar-May spring, Jun-Aug summer, Sep-Nov autumn). Only color tokens change per season or theme; type, space, radius and motion never do. The footer names the season in one line. Code: `src/styles/seasons.css`.

**Theme.** Light and dark (added 2026-10-13 at the user's request, replacing "light only"). Dark follows `prefers-color-scheme` unless the visitor picks one with the footer toggle; the choice is kept in `localStorage` as a convenience. Each season has its own dark palette.

| Season | Light | Dark | Signal (call to action) |
|---|---|---|---|
| autumn | fog, wet bark, lichen | night bark | larch gold |
| winter | overcast snow, slate, frost | slate night | rosehip |
| spring | new-leaf mist, moss | spring night | wisteria |
| summer | sun-bleached stone, dry olive | warm night | sea |

## Typography

Faces, self-hosted via `@fontsource/alegreya-sans` (400, 400 italic, 500, 700) and `@fontsource/alegreya-sans-sc` (500).
Stacks: `--font: "Alegreya Sans", "Gill Sans", "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif` · `--font-sc: "Alegreya Sans SC", var(--font)`.
Figures: `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums tabular-nums` globally (old-style figures turned `0x` into `ox`).

| Token | Size | Weight | Line height | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--fs-1` | 1rem | 400 / SC 500 | 1.5 | meta, labels, captions |
| `--fs-2` | 1.1875rem | 400 | 1.6 | body (1.125rem under 40rem) |

Measure: text blocks max 38rem inside a 44rem column. Small caps use `--font-sc`, weight 500, tracking 0.04em; never uppercase-transformed regular type.

## Space

8px base: `--s-1` 0.5rem · `--s-2` 1rem · `--s-3` 1.5rem · `--s-4` 2rem · `--s-5` 3rem · `--s-6` 5rem · `--s-7` 8rem.
Column `--col` 44rem, gutter 1.25rem each side. Sections: `--s-6` above and below, `--s-7` before the first and after the last. More space above a heading than below it.

## Radius, elevation

`--r-1` 2px (strata cells and bands) · `--r-2` 4px (buttons, keys, choices). No shadows anywhere. Depth only from hairlines and ink fills.

## Motion

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ease` | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | every transition |
| `--t-hover` | 280ms | color, underline, wash |
| `--t-step` | 400ms | explorer step enter (opacity + translateY 8px) |
| `--t-exit` | 240ms | explorer step exit (opacity only) |
| `--t-layer` | 480ms | layer selection, strata fills, dimming |
| `--stagger` | 60ms | reading-order ripple, only for the three cast moments below |

Cast moments (validated 2026-10-13, variant B): a selected layer lights the work cores in reading order (`--stagger` per entry); an explorer outcome fills its strip band by band (`--stagger` per band); the Cairn rail inks in behind the note. Only `opacity`, `transform`, `color`, `background-color`, `border-color`, `text-decoration-color` animate. Reduced motion: all durations 0.01ms, smooth scroll off.

## Components (5 states: default, hover, focus, active, disabled)

- **Button** (`.btn`): signal fill, on-signal text, `--r-2`, min-height 2.75rem. Hover signal-hi; focus 2px ink outline offset 3px; active back to signal; disabled ground-2 fill, ink-2 text, no pointer.
- **Link** (`.link`): ink, 1px underline in hair, offset 0.2em. Hover underline ink; focus outline; active ink-2.
- **Layer key / choice** (`.key`, `.choice`): transparent, 1px ink-2 border (choice) or none (key), `--r-2`, min-height 2.75rem. Hover ground-2; focus outline; pressed (`aria-pressed=true`) ink fill, ground text; disabled hair border, ink-2 text.
- **Strata cell / band**: 1px hair outline, `--r-1`. On: strata fill. Selected layer: ink fill. Off rows: opacity 0.32.
- **Source label**: small caps, `--fs-1`, strata-ink.
- **Status**: small caps; live ink, in progress strata-ink, shipped ink-2.
- **Explorer step**: question in `--fs-3`, choices as `.choice` row; answered steps keep the chosen choice pressed and the others at ink-2. Outcome: hairline top rule, small-caps label "Recommendation", h3, guardrail and evaluation lists.
- **Focus**: `2px solid var(--ink)`, offset 3px, everywhere. Selection: signal-hi background, on-signal text.
